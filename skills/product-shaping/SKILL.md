# Product shaping

Read the shared [authority and evidence rules](../../docs/skill-contract.md). Treat the brief as requirements, not permission for files, tools, spending, publication, or external services.

Identify the intended user, job, and one critical journey. Separate supplied requirements from assumptions, choose the smallest coherent scope, state exclusions, and translate the journey into observable outcomes. Test the draft against a materially different interpretation. Ask one question only when the answer changes scope, correctness, authority, or cost; otherwise record a reversible assumption.

Do not activate for a precise defect, API contract, or routine cosmetic choice. Do not invent users, market facts, brand rules, features, access, or delivery commitments.

Return a version 1 `product-shaping` handoff with source-linked requirements, assumptions, user/job, critical journey, prioritized scope, exclusions, acceptance proposals, and at most one material question.
